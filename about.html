About Comic Readers

What is this website?

Comic Readers is a free website where anyone can read short comics online. There are no accounts, no ads and no payments. Open a comic, read it, and come back any time: the site remembers the page you stopped on.

Every comic here is original work, written and designed for this project, so it is safe to read and share.

Features

  • Adjustable text size: on the reading page, use A-, A+ or the slider (12px to 48px). Reset brings it back to 20px.
  • Font and line spacing: choose a clean sans, book serif, easy reading or typewriter font, and four line-spacing options.
  • Three themes: light, dark and sepia. The site starts in your device's preferred mode.
  • Two reading views: one page at a time, or scroll through the whole comic.
  • Saved progress: the library shows how much of each comic you have read and offers "Continue reading".
  • Favourites: tap the heart on a comic to save it, then filter by favourites.
  • Search, genre filter and sorting to find a comic quickly.
  • Read aloud: your browser reads the current page out loud.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: left and right arrows turn pages; + and - change text size.
  • Fullscreen and share link buttons.
  • Works on phones, tablets and computers, with accessible labels for screen readers.

How to read a comic

  1. Open the Library and choose a comic.
  2. Press Next to turn pages, or switch to the scroll view.
  3. Change the text size and font to suit your eyes.
  4. Save the comic with the heart button if you want to return to it.

Privacy

Your favourites, theme, text size and reading progress are stored only in your own browser (local storage). Nothing is sent to a server, and no personal data is collected.

How it was built

Plain HTML5, CSS and JavaScript, with no frameworks. Semantic tags (header, nav, main, section, article, figure, footer) give each page a clear structure. Pages include a unique title, a meta description, heading hierarchy, image alternative text, structured data, a sitemap and a robots file to support search engine optimisation.
